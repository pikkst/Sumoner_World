# Consent: Sumoner World family test sessions

Organiser: **Eventnexus OÜ**, owner of *Sumoner World* (registry code: [________], contact: [________])

Sumoner World is a private game in early development. It is played only at home, on the family's own computer, and is not
public. This form covers every test session until it is withdrawn. Three parts:
**A** for adults who play, **B** for a parent or guardian, for each child, **C** the words read to each child before they play.

---

## What happens in a session
- You play an early version of the game for **10–20 minutes** (younger children: as long as they enjoy it, at most 15 minutes),
  then answer a few short questions. A parent is always in the room with a child.
- **The game is an early prototype.** It may crash or look unfinished. That is expected.
- **Content.** Players meet animal-like creatures. They can feed them, free them from snares, and also hit them; a creature
  that is hit three times dies, and the others may become scared or sad. There is no blood. A parent can choose to skip this.
- **Breaks and stopping.** Anyone may pause or stop at any time, without giving a reason. Nothing bad happens if they stop.

## What is recorded
| What | Always? | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| An anonymous game log: game events only (for example "a creature was fed"), a random session number and times. No name, no account, no information about the computer | yes | on the family PC, in the game's data folder |
| Answers to the questions (written down by the parent for young children) | yes | on paper or on the family PC |
| A screen recording with game sound | **only if ticked below** | on the family PC |
| The player's voice (microphone) in the recording | **only if ticked below** | on the family PC |
| Camera video of the player's face | **never** | – |

## How the information is used and kept
- Only to improve the game. Only Eventnexus OÜ (the game's creator) sees it.
- Results are written up without names. Children are never named or shown in anything about the game.
- Recordings and game logs are deleted **90 days after the session**. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time, and it is
  done within 7 days.
- Nothing is sent over the internet. The game runs against a server on the same PC.

## Part C: The child's agreement (read aloud, in the child's own language)
The parent reads this to the child in simple words and checks they understood. **If the child says no, or looks unsure, they do
not play.** A "no" is always fine.

> "We are making a game, and it isn't finished yet. Would you like to try it and tell us what you think? There are no right or
> wrong answers, and you can't do it wrong. If something is broken or boring, that helps us. You can stop whenever you want,
> just say so, and nobody will be upset. [If recording: The computer will record the game screen (and what you say), and only
> we will watch it.] Would you like to play?"
