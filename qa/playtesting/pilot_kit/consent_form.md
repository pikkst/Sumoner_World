# Consent form: Sumoner World playtest

> **DRAFT, not approved for use.** The stakeholder must approve this text (it is a legal document) before the first session.
> Before approval, fill in: the organiser's legal name and contact, any payment or reward, and the language the tester reads
> best (an Estonian version is needed for Estonian testers). If testers are paid or recruited through a third party, check
> whether more is needed.

**What this is.** You are invited to play an early prototype of the video game *Sumoner World* for about 15 minutes and answer a
few questions. The whole session takes about 30 minutes. We are testing the game, not you.

**Who organises it.** [Organiser's name, contact e-mail]

**What we record.**
- A screen recording of the game, with game sound and your microphone (what you say while playing).
- [Remote sessions only: the video call, which may show your face if your camera is on. You may keep your camera off.]
- An anonymous game log: what happened in the game (for example "a creature was fed"), with a random session number. It contains
  no name, account or information about your computer.
- Your answers to the questionnaire.

**What we use it for.** Only to improve the game. Results are reported as numbers and anonymous quotes. Nothing in a report
names you or shows your face or voice.

**Who sees it.** The test moderator and up to three people on the team who review the recordings.

**How long we keep it.** Recordings and logs are deleted 90 days after your session. Anonymous numbers and quotes in the report
may be kept.

**Your choices.**
- Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time, without giving a reason [and still receive the reward].
- You can ask us to delete your recording within the 90 days: write to the contact above with the date of your session.
- Please don't share what you saw in the game or what we asked, since others may test it too.

**Risks.** The game is an early prototype and may crash. It shows creatures being hurt or killed if the player chooses to do
that. If you are prone to motion sickness or photosensitive seizures, tell the moderator before you start.
