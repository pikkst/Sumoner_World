## Core Features Analysis

### Gameplay Mechanics
1. **Taming dinosaurs and creatures** (knock out + feed, or passive taming)
   - Strength: the slow taming process makes each tame feel earned
   - Weakness: very grindy (hours per tame). Tames are stat blocks with no personality.
2. **Survival + base building + tribe PvP**
   - Strength: deep sandbox and emergent player politics
   - Weakness: toxic PvP (offline raiding, wipes) puts off casual players
3. **Breeding and stat mutation**
   - Strength: long-term achiever goals
   - Weakness: needs huge time investment and spreadsheets

### Unique Features
- Ridable dinosaurs and a huge creature roster from 10+ years of content
- Cross-platform mod support (CurseForge) on console and PC
- Many large maps (paid and free)

### Missing Features
- Creature personality, memory or relationships
- A real accessible / casual mode
- Stable performance on mid-range hardware

## Lessons for Our Project

### What to Emulate
1. **Seasonal creature roadmap**: new creatures make good update headlines
2. **Discount strategy**: the 23.5x sale uplift shows how price-sensitive this audience is
3. **Mod support** later (post-1.0) to extend the long tail

### What to Avoid
1. **Pay-to-tame creatures and re-buying the same game**: these damage goodwill
2. **Shipping a world that needs hardware few players have**
3. **Forced PvP / offline raiding**: it drives away casual players

### Opportunities to Differentiate
1. **Creatures with behaviour, not just stats**
2. **Fast, meaningful bonding** instead of hours of knockout-taming
3. **Runs well on mid-range PCs and Steam Deck**

## Competitive Response Prediction
**Likely Response**: none. ARK runs on its own roadmap and won't react to a new game.
**Threat Level**: **Medium**. It competes for the same survival-creature time budget, but its audience leans hardcore.
**Counter-Strategy**: appeal to lapsed ARK players who love creatures but hate the grind and the PvP.

## Key Takeaways
1. ARK proves people want long-term creature progression, but its grind and monetisation leave many players unhappy.
2. The audience is very price- and discount-sensitive.
3. Performance is a real differentiator in this genre.

### Strategic Implications
Target ARK's "lapsed PvE player" segment with accessible, well-optimised gameplay where creatures have real personalities.
